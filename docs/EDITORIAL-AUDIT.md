# Website editorial audit

Source: `main` at `a0e5d67124e67405ef43058034313abb93cee709`, PR #10 and the live My Story page, reviewed 23 October 2026. This audit governs the four independently reviewable changes requested by the owner; it does not authorize unrelated infrastructure or content publication.

## What makes the reference work

My Story earns its length. An opening question leads into a real event, a navigable sequence of seven chapters, then a personal invitation. Every chapter changes the question being asked. The page uses Manrope for clear structure and Instrument Serif for the reflective voice. Full forest openings, pale interludes and one dark turning point mark actual changes in the narrative. Facts, a labelled remembered exchange, contextual numbers and a small personal aside interrupt prose for a reason. The reading navigation helps orientation without replacing ordinary links. The distinctive quality comes from composition and specificity, not simply large italic words.

Keep its source HTML, page component and scoped CSS intact. Carry its hierarchy, restraint, human voice, narrow reading measures and purposeful variation into other pages. Do not copy its seven chapters, remembered dialogue, artifacts or progress indicator onto pages that do not need them.

## Reference lock and decision ledger

Primary direction: the owner's live My Story V2, supported by existing Dual Contrast tokens. Audience: prospective clients and collaborators seeking a clear understanding of Zain, his work and how to begin. The owner's requested sequence is invitation → introduction → journey → growth → services.

Refero's live style catalog returned `NO_SUBSCRIPTION`; no external style results were available. Bundled typography, motion, copywriting and accessibility craft references supplement the owner's explicit reference. No unrelated palette, typeface, stock photograph or design identity is introduced.

| Decision                                      | Source and bounded role                                    | Reason                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forest, pale and white sections               | My Story's narrative rhythm; existing semantic palette     | Mark a change in purpose, not alternate backgrounds mechanically  |
| Serif for reflection, Manrope for information | Approved typography; typography craft reference            | Contrast voices while keeping services and controls clear         |
| Native reading links on genuinely long pages  | Story chapter navigation; no-JS constraint                 | Orientation without a new client island or hidden content         |
| Contextual metrics beside responsibilities    | Story's scope artifact; verified brand data                | Numbers need their ownership and approximate context              |

| Clear action before optional drafting         | Existing Contact flow; conversion constraint               | Visitors can book or email without completing a form              |

All existing visual scenarios remain exact-pixel comparisons; add deliberate new page references and accept only individually inspected native PNGs. Preserve Story/header/footer references when unchanged. Keep actual CI, local checks, deployment evidence and live HTTP verification distinct in the release record.
